# Roundabout: The God-Forsaken Ring — Traps

Trap numbers share the quest numbering space. Gaps in the sequence are ideas that didn't make it into the game — those numbers are retired and must not be reused. Only numbered entries below are active traps.

-----

**5. Swinging Blade Pendulum (inert)**
Atmospheric only. The blade has already fired — it hangs motionless from the ceiling of the Lower Crypt, dried blood on the edge, the Keeper's skeleton beneath it. Pressure plate still depressed under the skeleton's weight. No longer a threat; no perception check; no mechanical interaction.

**8. Rope Snare — Ankle**
**Descriptor:** a loop of fine cord hidden under the dirt
A loop of fine cord hidden under loose dirt on a cave floor.
Triggering it yanks the player off their feet (no damage) and suspends them upside down from a ceiling hook for several turns unless they cut the rope or pass a strength check to pull free.
*(Quest 7 — The Bone Flute: Trap 8 guards the crawlspace off the Inscription Chamber in the mid-tier dungeon. Triggering it or spotting it with a perception check reveals the crawlspace entrance to the Cave Creature's Lair.)*
Built (2026-10-04): Level 6 perception on entry, every visit until found. Spotted → the player steps around it (counts as disarmed: 3 XP, Rogue +5). Not spotted → fires on entry. While hanging: `PULL FREE` / `STRUGGLE` (Level 4 strength, retry) or `CUT CORD` with a blade; no release on its own. Text in locations.md — Inscription Chamber.
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** was Level 2 perception / Level 2 strength, which never fired at level 6, the level players reach the mid tier at. Now it hangs 37% of level-6 Warriors and 17% of Mages and Rogues (79% / 51% at level 5).
- Pulling free is Level 4, not Level 5, so it can't be out of reach: a level-4 Mage or Rogue can't roll a Level 5 check's 24 and, without a blade, would hang for good. It passes 25% of the time even at level 4, and 93–98% at level 6.


**15. Magnetic Chest**
**Descriptor:** a lodestone embedded in the chest's lid, the latch ringed with metallic filings
A chest in the dungeon is rigged so opening it without disarming triggers a powerful magnetic pulse.
Any metal items in the player's inventory are yanked toward the chest and stuck to it — including equipped armor and weapons — requiring a strength check to pry each one loose, costing a turn per item.
Perception spots unusual metallic filings arranged in a ring around the chest's latch; disarming means removing a lodestone embedded in the lid's underside.
*(Magnetic Vault, mid-tier key side.)*
Built (2026-10-04): Level 6 perception, Level 5 disarm (`DISARM LODESTONE`), Level 4 strength per stuck item; the pulse fires once and pulls iron and steel only. Rules, item list and text in locations.md — Magnetic Vault.
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** was Level 2 for all three. It fires only if the chest is opened before the lodestone is spotted: 37% of level-6 Warriors and 17% of Mages and Rogues who open it straight away, 14% / 3% after one `SEARCH`.
- Prying each stuck item is Level 4 (as Trap 8's pull free, so it's never out of reach): about a turn each at level 6. The disarm (retries allowed) stays easy — 1.2–1.5 tries — so care pays.

**16. Mirror Shatter Trap**
**Descriptor:** a firing pin behind the mirror's frame, a crossbow cocked and ready
A mirror rigged with a crossbow behind the frame. Fires at any player passing through — visible or invisible.
Perception check (Level 4) spots the firing pin; disarm roll (Level 4, trap skill) disarms it before it fires; otherwise the crossbow fires once and is spent — 1 heart physical damage: *Something clicks behind the frame. A bolt cracks out of the dark and catches you before you can move.*
Either way, the trap is no longer a factor on the way out or on return visits.
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** raised from Level 2 / Level 2, which never fired at the level players arrive at. Most players reach the Chuckle House at level 4 or above (the walkthrough is built to reach level 4 for Lynds). It fires for 94% of level-4 Warriors, 86% of Mages and 70% of Rogues; at level 5, 20% / 18% / 6%; at level 6, 7% of Warriors and 2% of Rogues.
The mirror also repels visible players until the Chuckle House ghost is freed.
*(Chuckle House — The Shatter Trap Mirror.)*

**17. Smoke Bomb Cache**
**Descriptor:** a trigger hidden among an unstable arrangement of clay pots
(2026-10-14: was "an unstable arrangement of clay pots, one of them the trigger", whose inner comma broke the disarm line: *You spot … one of them the trigger and are able to disarm it*.)
A shelf of old clay pots in the dungeon supply room appears harmless. The smoke jar (Quest 24) is hidden among them.
Disturbing the shelf without disarming triggers one pot to smash, dealing 1 heart of **smoke damage** — and revealing the smoke jar, now visible among the wreckage.
Perception spots the unstable arrangement and identifies which pot is the trigger; disarming means carefully removing the trigger pot first, after which the smoke jar can be safely retrieved.
Either way the smoke jar is obtainable — the trap just makes it costly.
Fires on the first entry (trip mechanism in the floor). Perception **Level 5**, disarm **Level 4** (trap roll) — raised 2026-10-15 from Level 2 / Level 2 (TODO #17), the same as Trap 45: it fires for about half of level-5 Warriors, 30% of Mages, 20% of Rogues. Disarm: 3 XP (+5 Rogue).
Fired: *Your foot catches on something in the floor. On the shelf beside you a clay pot tips, falls and bursts, and the room fills with choking grey smoke. When it clears, your eyes are streaming and your chest aches.* — 1 heart smoke damage.
*(Supply Room, upper tier.)*

**19. Electrified Portcullis**
**Descriptor:** an arcane charge running through the portcullis, the discharge mechanism set into the wall beside it
A metal portcullis blocks a dungeon corridor, carrying an arcane charge — touching it without disarming deals 1 heart of **lightning damage** and stuns the player for one turn.
Three ways to pass:
(1) **Perception check** to spot the charge and locate the discharge mechanism in the wall.
(2) **Disarm check** to safely discharge it.
(3) **Strength check** to lift it — but only if the charge has been discharged first, otherwise lifting deals the arcane damage.
A **Portcullis Bar** is found in the dungeon supply room — no perception check needed there.
Once the portcullis is lifted, `USE PORTCULLIS BAR` props it open permanently.
Without the bar, the portcullis drops when released.
It blocks the corridor from both sides: coming up from the Shrine Room by the Quest 38 shortcut, the way north is shut until it's propped (locations.md — Portcullis Corridor, Either side).
**Retries (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** a failed check no longer leaves the portcullis charged for good.
- The Level 4 perception check runs on every visit until the mechanism is spotted, and `SEARCH` gives one more try per visit. Spotting it runs the Level 4 disarm straight away, as before (a botch shocks).
- Once spotted, while it's still charged, `DISARM PORTCULLIS` / `DISARM MECHANISM` retries the Level 4 disarm, as often as you like. Success discharges it and pays the trap's XP (5, Rogue +5). A failed retry costs the turn and nothing else (no shock).
- `TOUCH` and `LIFT PORTCULLIS` while charged both shock (1 heart, lose a turn).
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** Level 4 perception, Level 4 disarm, Level 4 strength to lift (all were Level 2, which never fired at level 5). Not higher: the portcullis guards the north route, and a level-4 Warrior can't roll a Level 5 check's 24, so a Level 5 perception check would close the route for good.
- On the first pass it goes unspotted or botched for 20% of level-5 Warriors, 18% of Mages and 6% of Rogues (94% / 86% / 70% at level 4); the retries keep it a cost in hearts and turns, never a wall. Lifting passes 25% of the time even for a level-4 Mage or Rogue.
All text in locations.md (Portcullis Corridor).
*(Portcullis Corridor, upper tier.)*

**29. Pressure Plate — Bell**
**A scripted event, not a trap to beat (2026-10-14, TODO #21).** The plate fires the first time the player enters the Combat Room, from either side. There's no perception check, no disarm, no Actually Enchanted Glasses choice and no XP. It rings a bell in the Creature Den, the door opens, and the Warden comes out to fight. The door stays open from then on.
It used to be spotted and disarmed, but the auto-disarm left the Den door shut, and the Guardian's Lantern behind it, for most level-4 warriors and nearly every Rogue. The number is kept. All text: locations.md (Combat Room).
*(Combat Room / Warden encounter, upper tier.)*

**33. Weight-Sensitive Pedestal**
A **Chachapoyan Fertility Idol** sits on a pressure-sensitive pedestal in the upper tier.
Removing it without replacing it with something of equal weight triggers a heavy stone door to slam shut behind the player — the north doorway only. The way south stays open, so the player can go and find the crowbar (Prayer Alcove) and come back: `PRY DOOR` with the crowbar, **Level 4** strength check, retries allowed.
`LIFT SLAB` / `RAISE SLAB` with the crowbar held is the same as `PRY DOOR` (text in locations.md, Idol Room).
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** perception Level 5, pry Level 4 (both were Level 2, which never missed at level 5). The plate goes unseen on a visit for 40% of level-5 Warriors and 15% of Mages and Rogues (16% / 2% after a `SEARCH`). The pry stays Level 4 so it's never out of reach (as Trap 8's pull free).
Perception **Level 5** (every visit until found) shows the pressure plate in the room description; once the idol is gone, that line says whether the plate fired or the salt holds it, and the room description shows the slab across the north doorway until it is pried. The safe swap counts as the disarm: 5 XP (+5 Rogue). `TIP IDOL` on the pedestal only nudges it (mechanics.md — Parser Verbs):
no trap.
Text in locations.md (Idol Room).
Safe swap requires placing a **sack of salt** (found in the supply room, described as "looks like it weighs as much as a Chachapoyan Fertility Idol") on the pedestal simultaneously with `SWAP IDOL WITH SALT`.
The idol is treasure — not required for the ring quest.
**Layout note:** The idol room is gated behind the supply room — the player must pass through the supply room first, ensuring the sack of salt is findable before the pedestal is encountered.
*(Idol Room, upper tier.)*

**36. Bone Crunch Floor**
**Descriptor:** a floor carpeted in dry bones, arranged to crack at the first step
A section of floor outside the skeleton room in the dungeon lower tier is covered with dry bones arranged so they crunch loudly when walked on.
The bones are plainly visible — no perception check required.
The room text warns explicitly: *"The sound is coming from beyond the south doorway — bone grinding on stone, steady and unhurried. You get the distinct impression that silence is not optional here."*
**Disarm:** `CLEAR BONES` sweeps a silent path through before moving to the next room — the only safe way to proceed.
**If triggered** (player moves toward the skeleton room without clearing first): the bones crack underfoot, the grinding stops, and the doorway fills. Instant death — no combat round.
`EAST` or `SOUTH` before clearing triggers it; `WEST` (the way in) is always safe. Room and `CLEAR BONES` text in locations.md (Antechamber).
The skeleton room beyond is a full room of undead skeletons, a dead end with nothing inside. It is never required to enter.
**Death text:** *"The bones crack underfoot. The grinding stops. Then the doorway fills."*
*(Antechamber → Skeleton Room, lower tier.)*

**41. Pressure Plate — Flooding**
**A scripted event, not a trap to beat (2026-10-14, TODO #22).** The plate fires on entry to the Flooding Room — the first and only visit. A slab drops behind the player (there's no way back south), and water pours in. No perception check, no disarm, no Actually Enchanted Glasses choice, no timer and no XP.
Three levers, each one pull: **Right** snaps off and more water surges in; **Middle** stops the water; **Left** opens the sluice and sweeps the player one-way down to the Spillway (the mid-tier trap side, Quest 50). No hint — after the middle one, the only thing left is to pull the others.
No damage on the sweep, except that any thin paper carried is destroyed (items.md — Thin Paper). The way back out is the apprentice's tunnel to Bog-NW (Quest 50) — there is no climbing back up.
It used to be spotted and disarmed with a two-turn flood, but the auto-disarm locked most level-4 warriors and nearly every Rogue out of the trap side. The number is kept. All text: locations.md (Flooding Room). *(Flooding Room, upper tier.)*

**45. Invisible Thread — Ink**
**Descriptor:** a near-invisible thread strung at chest height across the corridor
A thread at chest height across a dungeon corridor is tied to an ink bladder above.
Triggering it douses the player in black ink — no heart damage, but the player's appearance changes until they bathe.
**Bathing:** Included with inn rest at 5 Zenni.
**Ring effect:** Ink negates ring invisibility — the player is visible even while wearing the ring. Corruption still ticks normally while the ring is worn.
**Difficulty (decided 2026-10-15, TODO #17):** **Level 5** perception, then **Level 4** disarm (trap roll), on the first pass. It was Level 2 / Level 2, which never fired at level 5, the level players arrive at.
- Inked: about half of level-5 Warriors, 30% of Mages, 20% of Rogues; nearly every level-4 arrival (every Warrior — a Level 5 check is beyond level 4's highest roll; Mages and Rogues, with +3 perception, 96–98%); at level 6, 18% of Warriors and 5% of Rogues. (Exact; a 20,000-run engine simulation agrees.)
**NPC impacts while inked:**
- **May — rooms only:** `RENT ROOM` (inn rest and bath, 5 Zenni) is always available regardless of ink status, even at full hearts. It's the only thing May will do for an inked player. The player cannot be permanently stuck.
- **Refuse entirely:** May for everything but rooms (no hints, food or drink; her quest hand-overs wait until the player is clean), Shamus (no vendor sales), the trainers (Raznak, Redcrosse Knight), Litlock (no Dankhaus access), Records Room Worker (no charter), Librarian (refuses to assist), all active quest givers.
- **What a refusal blocks:** every action with that NPC — TALK, GIVE (the player keeps the item), BUY, PAY / GIVE Zenni, CHALLENGE / FIGHT, the trials. No Zenni taken; the refusal uses a turn, like any other. Reading Shamus's slate still works.
- **Refusal lines** (npcs.md has each): Shamus, Raznak, the Knight, Litlock (at the door, even when invited), the Records Room Worker and the Librarian have their own. The active quest givers share one: *{Name} takes one look at the ink and wants nothing to do with you until it's gone.*
- **Active quest givers:** the Archivist, the Child at the Old Oak, the Beekeeper, Ivanaar, Haalvar, Aylora, Pyronicus, Councilman Rowan Finch, Lynds. Not affected: Ty, the Afflicted Apprentice (and Kevry and the Boggart, below).
- **Arrivals and trials while inked:** quest givers' unprompted arrival lines hold until the player is clean — Ivanaar's first greeting (and Quest 57's discovery), Haalvar's riddle on entering the hut, the beekeeper's first greeting (and Quest 24's discovery); riddle answers get the shared line.
  Trial 3 (`DRINK` at the Fire Pit) gets the shared line as Aylora. Trial 2's stones still work (the unnamed child isn't a quest giver). May's unprompted introduction still fires.
- **Will Passion:** Still engages — including spell teaching; he doesn't refuse — but disdainful.
  The first thing the player does with him while inked gets his line, then goes ahead; after that he's normal until the player is inked again. `TALK TO WILL` gets the line every time while inked. Response: *"Will peers over his spectacles. 'And you found the thread,' he says, and pauses. 'Everyone finds the thread.' He turns back to his work. 'The inn has a bath. Use it. Twice.'"*
- **Chuckle House ghost:** Inaccessible while inked — ink negates ring invisibility so the ghost cannot be seen or interacted with.
- **Kevry:** Unaffected — too isolated to notice or care.
- **Toll Bridge Boggart:** Unaffected.
**The player's side** (2026-10-15, TODO #29; the v2.7 playtest found nothing that showed the ink or pointed at a bath):
- `EXAMINE ME` / `EXAMINE INK` (INK is a noun only while inked): *Ink, everywhere, drying in streaks. People are going to notice.* Clean, `EXAMINE ME`: *You look like someone who's been adventuring. That's the best that can be said.*
- `WASH` / `RINSE` / `SCRUB` (alone, `ME`, `INK`, `HANDS`, `IN [thing]`), `CLEAN ME` / `CLEAN INK`, and `SWIM` / `BATHE` anywhere: *It isn't coming off with anything you've got here. Somewhere with a bath, then.* Clean, the `WASH` forms: *You're clean enough.* One turn. Only the inn's bath (`RENT ROOM`) clears it.
Perception spots the thread's slight shadow on the wall; disarming cuts it cleanly. *(Ink Corridor, upper tier.)*
