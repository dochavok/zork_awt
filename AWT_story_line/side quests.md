# Enchanted Realms: One Ring — Side Quests

-----

**4. The Whispering Jar**
A cracked ceramic jar in the inn's back room hums faintly when no one is watching. The innkeeper says it used to call out warnings before the inn closed. To restore it, the player must bring a wax seal from the Town Hall Upper Hall (display cabinet — `OPEN CABINET`, `TAKE SEAL`; no key or perception check required) and a handful of silver dust found in the Mine Passage of the cave dungeon's mid-tier (perception check required). The restoration phrase is etched into the base of the jar — readable with a perception check or the Enchanted Glasses. With both materials in hand: `PRESS SEAL`, `DUST JAR`, `READ INCANTATION`. The jar whispers once — *"The ceiling of the thermal vent holds a secret."* — one time only, never repeated. **In the lower tier:** The Thermal Vent Room has fire clay pressed into the underside of a ceiling overhang, kept workable by rising heat. It is invisible until the player types `LOOK UP` — which reveals it. `TAKE CLAY` retrieves it. No perception check; no other hint anywhere in the game.

**7. The Bone Flute**
Pyronicus posts a notice on the inn quest board 20 turns after the player first meets him, asking for help recovering a bone flute stolen from him by a cave creature. The creature's lair is accessed through a tight crawlspace off the Inscription Chamber in the dungeon mid-tier. The crawlspace is revealed by Trap 8 (Rope Snare) — triggering it or spotting it with a perception check reveals the opening. No weight restriction on entry. Inside the Cave Creature's Lair: the creature is absent and never encountered — the lair is empty except for the bone flute on the floor. Returning the flute to Pyronicus earns a fireball spell scroll — castable in combat.

**12. The Locked Music Box**
In Will's Wizard Tower, a locked music box sits on a high shelf. Will mentions offhandedly that it belonged to a student who never returned — this is the quest discovery moment. Will provides hints directly; this quest is not on May's hint list. The key is hidden in Bog-NW — inside a hollow log found only on a successful perception check. Opening the box plays a short melody, and tucked inside is a scroll: the **Light spell**. Mages read the scroll to learn it directly (scroll consumed). Warriors/Rogues take it to Will Passion to learn (scroll consumed; cannot wear Enchanted Glasses in Will's presence). Light spell: duration 10 turns, reuse timer 20 turns. **Will's hints:** Triggered every time the player types `LOOK AT MUSIC BOX`. Will speaks unprompted — no dialogue command needed. Three progressive hints, each delivered on successive examinations: (1) Will mentions the box and the missing student wistfully — quest discovered. (2) Will recalls the student spent a lot of time in the bog. (3) Will specifically remembers the student liked to hide things in hollow logs. After all three hints are given, subsequent `LOOK AT MUSIC BOX` repeats the third hint only. Once the Light spell scroll has been found, `LOOK AT MUSIC BOX` triggers no hint — Will says nothing further about it. Hints are not purchased — they are free and triggered by examination.

**17. The Frozen Watch**
The Records Room Worker has posted on the Quest Board seeking news of a missing relative who entered the Chuckle House and never returned, and asking for the relative's pocket watch to be recovered if they are dead. The relative is dead — his ghost haunts the Chuckle House, hostile, trying to keep everyone out and the watch out of anyone's hands. The player must wear the One Ring throughout the Chuckle House (all mirrors repel visible players). Casting `UNBIND UNDEAD` on the ghost releases him; he says *"Thank you. I can rest now."* and drops the pocket watch. The player delivers the watch to the Records Room Worker, who gives up the town charter as thanks. **Spell source:** Unbind Undead scroll on Silas's desk at the Lighthouse — learned via standard spell mechanic (Mages directly; scroll for Warriors/Rogues to take to Will). **Reward:** Town charter → required for Quest 27 (Toll Bridge/dungeon access). **Corruption note:** Wearing the ring throughout the Chuckle House is an intentional corruption cost.

**19 & 30. The Hollow Statue / The Undead Warden**
A stone statue in the town square has a hollow base — a perception check reveals a faint seam around the pedestal. Forcing it open with a crowbar reveals a small compartment containing a **silver stake** and a folded note. The note is written in the hand of the Keeper of the Faiths — the priest who oversaw the Church of All. It warns that something dangerous dwells in the passages below Roundabout, and that the stake is the only means of dealing with it. The note is unsigned but the emerald wax seal identifies its author. The locked door to the Keeper's Chamber in the Church of All gives no indication the Keeper is missing — it is simply locked.

**Discovery — Path A (The Scholar):** Talking to the Librarian about *"the Veil of the Arcane"* or *"the lower passages"* reveals what she knows. May's tier 1 hint directs players to the library: *"There's talk of a scholar who went into the deep passages and never came back. The Librarian might know something about it."*

**Library — Room Description:**
*The library is smaller than the building suggests from outside — half the floor space is shelving, floor to ceiling, packed in columns with narrow gaps between. A card catalogue occupies one wall. The other holds a reading table, lamp burning low, a cup of something gone cold.*

*The librarian looks up when you enter. Unlike every librarian you have ever imagined, she appears to want to talk to you.*

**TALK TO LIBRARIAN — First interaction:**

She sets down what she's holding and folds her hands on the desk. Her voice arrives in pieces — tones and rhythms that don't quite match, stitched together at the seams.

*"I have been waiting for* someone *to come asking."*

She pulls a thin ledger from under the desk without looking for it.

*"'The Veil of the Arcane — I need access to whatever you have.' That's what he said. First thing. Didn't introduce himself. I didn't ask."*

She opens the ledger to a marked page.

*"He was here for —* weeks. *Every day. 'Have you found anything else? Anything older?' He was very — 'I think I've found something. I think I know where it is.' "*

She closes the ledger.

*"And then he stopped coming. I checked the application. There's a date. There's no — 'I'll be back by —' there's no return date. There never was."*

She looks at you steadily.

*"'The lower passages. That's where it will be. That's where everything that old ends up.' "*

She sets the ledger aside.

**TALK TO LIBRARIAN — Subsequent visits (before werewolf defeated):**

*"'The lower passages.' That's all I have. That's the last thing."*

**TALK TO LIBRARIAN — After werewolf defeated:**

She looks at you for a moment when you come in.

*"'It's not a dead end — it's* a beginning.' "*

She picks up her book.

*"He said that once, about a research problem. I think about it."*

**Discovery — Path B (The Keeper):** May's hints reveal the Keeper's disappearance in three tiers:
- Tier 1: *"The Keeper of the Faiths has gone missing. No one's seen him in some time."*
- Tier 2: *"Word is he went looking for someone — a scholar who'd gone into the deep passages and never come back."*
- Tier 3: *"No one can get into his office at the Church. Locked tight. You'd need his key to find out what he knew."*

Both paths point toward the lower tier. Neither reveals what became of the scholar or the Keeper.

In the lower tier, the player finds the Keeper's skeleton in the Lower Crypt. A large curved blade hangs motionless overhead, dried blood on its edge. The skull is split. Among the remains: a ring of keys and robes identifying him as a Keeper — the same emerald seal as on the statue note. He never made it past the first trap.

The key opens the Keeper's Chamber in the Church of All. Inside: sparse quarters, personal effects, a **vial of holy water** on the writing desk, and a note in the Keeper's hand. The note describes what he found below — the scholar had been transformed into an undead werewolf by the ancient magic he was studying. The Keeper prepared the holy water to consecrate the stake but descended before he could use it. The note explains that the stake must be consecrated with holy water to be effective against the creature.

`POUR HOLY WATER ON STAKE` produces a **consecrated silver stake**.

The undead werewolf is encountered deeper in the lower tier. It cannot be harmed by conventional weapons. `DRIVE STAKE INTO WEREWOLF` with the consecrated stake destroys it. The **Ivory Torch** is mounted on the wall of the werewolf's room.

**Werewolf drops:**
- **The Werewolf's Amulet** — a tarnished amulet bearing the seven-pointed star of **The Veil of the Arcane**; a ring ritual artifact consumed at the Church of All altar

**22. The Ruined Aqueduct**
A section of the old stone aqueduct that feeds the town has collapsed, leaving a gap three rooms wide in the cave dungeon's upper tier. The player finds stone blocks in a collapsed gallery nearby and must carry them two at a time (each one costs a strength check not to drop mid-transit) to the gap. Placing all three blocks and sealing the joints with mortar from the supply room restores the flow. The innkeeper, grateful for improved water quality, permanently lowers the cost of Food & Drink by one Zenni. **Cascade:** Once the aqueduct is repaired, the fountain in Roundabout Town Square begins running — its water is required to mix the clay adhesive for Quest 49 (The Ruined Shrine). **Quest Board:** Posted early — one of the first quests available, ensuring most players complete it naturally before needing the fountain.

**24. The Beekeeper's Swarm**
A beekeeper on the forest edge is trying to recapture an escaped swarm that's nested in a hollow tree near the bog. The swarm can't be approached without provoking it — unless the player first finds a smoke jar (a small sealed container with a smoldering rag inside) in the dungeon supply room. Holding the smoke jar and walking into the swarm room pacifies the bees. The player then takes the queen — a small tagged bee in a glass vial near the nest — back to the beekeeper, earning a jar of enchanted honey that restores two hearts when consumed.

**25. The Flooded Cellar**
The innkeeper mentions a locked cellar beneath the inn that flooded years ago and was sealed off. She'll give the player the cellar key if they can unclog the drain. The cellar is accessed through a cellar door in the kitchen — the bartender holds the key. Removing the drain cover requires the crowbar from the dungeon. The clog itself is a clot of old debris that yields to the player typing `clear drain` after removing the cover. Once drained, the cellar contains a cache of pre-flood Zenni. The bartender rewards the player with a pair of boots — delivering them with a line along the lines of "forgot I had these, should have given them to you BEFORE you cleaned up the cellar." A door to the Secret Tunnel is revealed; it can be opened or closed and stays in whatever state the player leaves it. Travel is freely bidirectional once drained. Entering the flooded cellar from either direction before draining is an instant drowning fail state with no warning. **Open question:** Should entering from the kitchen side trigger a perception check to notice the flood before stepping in, or always a surprise? Leaning no roll — resolve later. **Boots reward:** Leg-slot armor with a **+3 trap disarm roll bonus** — the bartender's joke about giving them before the cleaning lands better if the boots are actually useful for dungeon work.

**27. The Toll Bridge Operator**
A Boggart has taken up residence at a small bridge deep in the Secret Tunnels, claiming squatter's rights and charging an extortionate toll. The bridge is the only route to the Dungeon Entrance. The Boggart gives no hints about what would satisfy him — he's simply gruff, legally creative, and immovable. A perception check on the bridge reveals a faded official seal showing the bridge was public property — this triggers quest discovery and unlocks May's hint. **May's hint (one tier only):** *"There's something in the town charter about public bridges and rights of way. Might be worth a look."* No Quest Board entry for this quest. **If the player already has the charter when they find the bridge:** *"You remember something about a bridge in the town charter."* `GIVE CHARTER TO BOGGART` forces him to stand down — he reads it carefully, grumbles, and vacates. He drops his strongbox as he leaves — which the player can open with a strength check to recover several turns' worth of Zenni. Bridge is permanently free after this.

**28. The Archivist's Request**
An archivist in the town library is trying to authenticate an old map but needs a physical rubbing of a specific stone engraving in the dungeon — the Inscription Chamber in the mid-tier bears an ancient inscription. The player must buy thin paper from a vendor (2 Zenni; paper is destroyed if the player gets wet — it reappears for sale at the vendor) and find a stick of charcoal in the Mine Passage (mid-tier). `RUB PAPER ON ENGRAVING` in the Inscription Chamber produces the rubbing. Bringing it to the archivist earns an incantation scroll — the archivist notes: *"This appears to be an answer to a question I was never able to find."* The scroll is required to open the speaking door in the dungeon lower tier (Quest 34). No hints from May; no quest board posting.

**32. The Missing Gravestone**
**Councilman Rowan Finch** approaches the player — a gravestone marking the grave of his grandfather **Calder Finch** was stolen from the cemetery. A perception check in the bog reveals the stone face-down in the mud. Attempting to pick it up returns: *"You'll need a way to carry this."* The player must find a hand cart in the cave dungeon's storage area and use `load stone onto cart` to move it back to town. **Reward interaction:** *"That cart came from the dungeon," Rowan says flatly. "Don't bother denying it — I've seen it before, in my grandfather's papers." He studies you for a moment. "He left a key. Said it led to a lower level — wouldn't say what was down there." He holds it out. "Take it. It was never meant for me."* **Reward: Middle Tier Key** — gates access to the key side of the mid-tier dungeon. Calder Finch's gravestone is returned to the cemetery. **Discovery trigger:** Finding the Mid-Tier Key Door in the dungeon triggers quest discovery and unlocks May's three-tier hints. No Quest Board posting. **May's hints:** Tier 1: *"There's a locked door down in the tunnels, they say. Heavy thing — needs a proper key. Word is someone in town might know something about it."* Tier 2: *"The Finch family goes back a long way in Roundabout. Rowan's grandfather was quite the explorer, from what I hear. Rowan works up at the Town Hall if you want to ask him about it."* Tier 3: *"Old Calder Finch — now there was an explorer. Spent more time underground than above it. Whatever he knew about that door went with him to the cemetery. Rowan might point you in the right direction."* **Gravestone hint (one tier only):** *"I've heard carts are good for moving heavy things. Stones, for instance."*

**34. The Frozen Soldier**
Deep in the cave dungeon's lower tier, a perception check reveals that a section of wall is not a wall at all — it is a door. The door speaks when approached, asking a question in an unknown language. The question is clearly a question, but cannot be understood. `READ SCROLL` (incantation scroll from Quest 28) causes the door to open — the scroll is the answer to the door's unknowable question. No hints anywhere; the archivist's remark when handing over the scroll (*"an answer to a question I was never able to find"*) and the door's spoken question are the only two pieces the player must connect. Behind the door stands a soldier encased in a block of magical ice — alive but suspended. The block can be thawed by carrying the ivory torch from the dungeon's lower level and holding it adjacent to the ice for two turns (`HOLD TORCH NEAR ICE` both turns). The soldier, revived, gives the player his sword — a named blade with a combat bonus — and wanders off dazed. He reappears later in town as a minor NPC who gives free weapon training sessions.

**38. The Collapsed Passage**
A partially collapsed tunnel in the cave dungeon has timbers wedged across it, blocking a secondary exit that would significantly speed up dungeon traversal. Clearing the timbers requires a pickaxe (found in the mine, not the dungeon) and three separate strength checks — one for each main timber. After each success, another timber loosens. After clearing all three and propping the passage with a support beam found in the dungeon's storage area, the shortcut becomes permanent. The passage exits into the Rickety Bridge, placing the player between the Shrine Room and the Mid-Tier Key Door — bypassing everything above.

**40. Shamus's Recipe**
Shamus mentions wistfully that he once made a healing stew that restored two hearts instead of one, but he's missing a key ingredient: bog thyme, a herb that grows only in one specific room of the bog (revealed by a perception check). The player must also bring back a small clay pot from the cave dungeon to cook it in, since Shamus's own pots are all cracked. Delivering both earns a permanent menu upgrade: the inn now sells a "hearty stew" option that restores two hearts for a modest Zenni premium. **Quest Board:** Posted early — one of the first quests available on the board.

**41. The Child's Kite**
A child at The Old Oak (a room between Beach Road Fork and Roundabout Forest) has lost a kite tangled in the oak's upper branches. `CLIMB TREE` retrieves the kite. As it comes loose, something tied to its tail slips free and drops to the ground: *a small flat stone, dark and smooth, threaded on a cord. Mineral veins run through it in a pattern that looks almost intentional.* This is a rune stone — `TAKE RUNE STONE` picks it up. `GIVE KITE TO CHILD` completes the quest. No perception check required anywhere in this quest. The rune stone is the reward — the child doesn't know it was there and doesn't want it. (See Quest 42 — one of three rune stones needed by the runesmith.)

**42. The Runesmith's Commission**
A runesmith has set up shop in town but lacks the raw materials to inscribe armor. He needs three rune stones — smooth flat rocks with natural mineral veins. Each looks different but is identified as a rune stone in inventory:
- **Bog rune stone:** *A grey stone, heavy for its size, one face worn flat by water. Faint lines are etched across the surface in no pattern you recognize.* Found in the bog (perception check).
- **Dungeon rune stone:** *A pale stone, roughly square, with deep natural veins of darker mineral running through it like old script.* Found in the Inscription Chamber, mid-tier dungeon (perception check).
- **Old Oak rune stone:** *A small flat stone, dark and smooth, threaded on a cord. Mineral veins run through it in a pattern that looks almost intentional.* Reward from Quest 41 (The Child's Kite).
Delivering all three earns the player a free runic inscription on any one piece of worn armor, permanently improving that armor's associated roll bonus by a small margin.

**49. The Ruined Shrine**
In Roundabout Forest, a perception check reveals a crumbled shrine to **The Verdant Circle**. The offering bowl is smashed — three pieces scattered across the world: one in the forest (near the shrine), one in the bog, one in the dungeon upper tier. Reassembling requires a clay adhesive — fire clay found in the dungeon lower tier, mixed with water from the Town Square fountain. The fountain only runs after Quest 22 (The Ruined Aqueduct) is completed. Once assembled, the repaired bowl is placed back on the shrine pedestal with a Zenni coin offering. **The repaired bowl is the third ring ritual artifact** — consumed at The Verdant Circle ritual at the Church of All altar. The player must be attuned to The Verdant Circle on the altar dial to perform the ritual. **Quest flow:** Find shrine → collect three pieces → descend to lower tier for fire clay → return to fountain to mix adhesive → assemble bowl → return to shrine → bowl becomes artifact → perform ritual at altar. **Mixing failure text:** If the player attempts to mix the clay adhesive while the fountain is dry: *"The fountain is dry. You'll need clean running water."* **May's hints:** Tier 1: *"Something about that shrine bowl needs water — clean, running water. Check the Quest Board; there may be something relevant posted."* Tier 2: *"The town fountain hasn't run in years. Word is the aqueduct beneath the dungeon needs repair — it's on the Quest Board if you haven't seen it."*

**50. The Lost Apprentice**
Will Passion posts a notice on the inn quest board — anonymously, no name given, just a description of a missing person last seen heading toward the dungeon. **The posting is removed permanently if the player disarms the Flooding Room trap** (Trap 41) — those players will never reach the trap side and Quest 50 becomes inaccessible. Fully missable; no impact on the ring quest. The apprentice is found in the Lost Apprentice's Cell on the trap side of the mid tier — alive, but afflicted by something in the dungeon that has turned him feral and frightened, unrecognizable as himself. The player must defeat him in combat, which breaks the affliction. Coming around, he is disoriented and sad — he'd been lucid enough to dig upward before losing himself. The hole in the cell floor is nearly through to the surface. With the shovel, the player finishes it, creating the permanent Bog-NW exit. The apprentice follows the player out through the hole. As they surface together in the bog, he pulls off his gloves and hands them over — he doesn't have much else to give. Quest completes on bog exit — no return to Will required. **Reward: Apprentice's Gloves** — hands slot, +2 or +3 combat roll bonus (TBD once dice system designed). Will is visibly shaken when the player next visits the tower; he says little about it.
