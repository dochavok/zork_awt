ROUNDABOUT: THE GOD-FORSAKEN RING
Minimal Ring Quest Walkthrough
================================
Fewest commands to complete the three binding rituals and hear Will's
"Well done." Sections map one-to-one with design requirements; a failing
test section identifies exactly which requirement is broken.

Test runner rules:
  > lines     : feed verbatim to game.do_turn()
  (input: X)  : fed via patched input() during character creation
  [fragment]  : required case-insensitive substring in stdout
  Prose lines : annotation only — ignored by the test runner

Because this is a Warrior walkthrough, the Warrior starts with melee
skill. Enchanted Glasses are retrieved in section B (Will's Bedroom) —
as early as possible, before sailing, so Kevry can enchant them on the
way to the Pale Blade chain and before the ring is retrieved from
Pyronicus. Actually Enchanted Glasses pass all perception checks,
removing randomness from the rest of the walkthrough.

=============================================================================
A) Opening — White House & Will's Briefing
=============================================================================
The game starts west of the white house. Any command except OPEN MAILBOX
returns the Zork joke. Opening the mailbox triggers Will's appearance
and character creation. After class and name, examining the painting
teleports the player to the Tale and Ale.

> OPEN MAILBOX                [mailbox opens]
(input: yes)                 [Then we begin]
(input: warrior)             [You have chosen: Warrior]
(input: Tester)              [Welcome, Tester]

Will hands over 10 Zenni and the ring briefing fires. The painting on
the wall is the exit. Examining it teleports us to the Tale and Ale.

> EXAMINE PAINTING            [Tale and Ale]

=============================================================================
B) Enchanted Glasses — Will's Bedroom
=============================================================================
The hidden bedroom inside the tower is gated by a silent perception
check. To avoid relying on that random check we go back to the tower via
the mailbox in the tavern Main Room and wait for the check to succeed.
We need the glasses before sailing so Kevry can enchant them on the way.

Return to the tower:

> OPEN MAILBOX                [tower]

The bedroom door becomes visible on a successful Easy perception check
that fires every visit. Since these are integration tests with
always-max random rolls, the check succeeds immediately.

> NORTH                       [nightstand]

Take the glasses.

> TAKE GLASSES                [wire-rimmed glasses]
> SOUTH                       [Will's Wizard Tower]
> EXAMINE PAINTING            [Tale and Ale]
> WEAR GLASSES                [glasses]

C1) Before Sailing — Scroll, Kite & the Pie Rat Heist
=============================================================================
Kevry's island is a way to the east across the Open Ocean, and the Pie
Rats won't let us aboard their ship without a disguise. This section
gets everything needed to board, picking up a few things on the way.

D) Ring Retrieval — Pyronicus's Forge
=============================================================================
Pyronicus is in his forge below the illusory volcano, east of town
through the Wasteland. The volcano requires a perception check to see
the entrance — auto-pass with Actually Enchanted Glasses. We need a
torch to enter dark areas (the forge itself is naturally lit, but the
path through the Secret Tunnels will need light later).

We already bought a torch from Shamus. Let's head east.

> WEST                        [Roundabout Beach]
> NORTH                       [Beach Road]
> WEST                        [Roundabout Wasteland]
> SOUTH                       [Volcano]
> DOWN                        [Pyronicus's Forge]
> TALK TO PYRONICUS           [ring]

Pyronicus hands over the ring willingly and gives the briefing. The
ring is now in inventory.


Return to Will with the ring for the second briefing (Church of All,
three faiths, three artifacts).

> UP                          [Volcano]
> NORTH                       [Roundabout Wasteland]
> WEST                        [Main East]

Return to Will's tower via the Tale and Ale mailbox.

> WEST                        [Town Square]
> SOUTH                       [Tale and Ale]
> REMOVE GLASSES              [glasses removed]
> OPEN MAILBOX                [tower]
> GIVE RING TO WILL           [three faiths]

Will explains the Church of All altar, the dial, and that three
religions are needed. He does not name which three. The second briefing
also posts Quest 17 (The Frozen Watch) on the Quest Board.

Warriors can't read spell scrolls alone. Reading the Unbind Undead
scroll in Will's presence has him teach it (the glasses came off
before the mailbox — wearing them in front of Will is an instant fail). The scroll
is consumed; the spell is ours for the Chuckle House ghost later.

> READ SCROLL                 [The knowing, I mean]

=============================================================================
The Pale Blade is forged from runed metal earned after completing all
three Viking trust trials. The Viking Encampment is west of the Archery
Range (reached by going west from Main West).

We need the torch for the dungeon later but the overworld is naturally
lit. Head to the encampment: north to Town Square, west through Main

> NORTH                       [Bog of Eternal Stench]
> TAKE BOWL PIECE             [You take the bowl piece]

OPTIONAL — not required to complete the ring. Done for XP so the

Bog-NE adds their exploration XP without backtracking.

G) Werewolf's Amulet — Silver Stake from Town Square Statue
=============================================================================
The undead werewolf in the lower dungeon drops the Werewolf's Amulet.
To kill it we need a consecrated silver stake: silver stake (statue
base) + holy water (Keeper's Chamber) + crowbar (Prayer Alcove, dungeon
upper tier). We'll collect items in the order they appear on the path.

First: the silver stake lives in the hollow base of the Town Square
statue. The seam is visible to anyone who examines it — no perception
check. A crowbar is required to pry it open.

Return to town:

> UP                          [Volcano]
> NORTH                       [Roundabout Wasteland]
> WEST                        [Main East]
> WEST                        [Town Square]
> LOOK AT STATUE              [seam]

We need the crowbar first (it's in the Prayer Alcove, dungeon upper
tier). We'll come back to the statue after getting the crowbar.

H1) Quest 59 — Beat Lynds (Heart Necklace, Dankhaus Access)
=============================================================================
The Chuckle House holds the ghost whose pocket watch earns the town
charter, and the charter clears the Toll Bridge into the dungeon. The
Chuckle House only becomes visible after Litlock's bonk, and Litlock's
home (the Dankhaus) is warded until Lynds invites us. So the chain
starts with Lynds, here in the Tale and Ale Main Room.

Arm wrestling is a single contested strength roll (ties reroll). At
level 4 a Warrior wins about 77% of the time; always-max rolls win.

SAVE first. A loss means a 20-turn wait before Lynds will go again —
on a ring-only run, RESTORE and challenge him again instead.

> SAVE                        [Saved]
> CHALLENGE LYNDS             [Heart Necklace]
> WEAR NECKLACE               [necklace]

Lynds's invitation clears the Dankhaus wards.

=============================================================================
I1) Dungeon Upper Tier — Down to the Mid-Tier Key Door (Crowbar)
=============================================================================
The Ink Corridor is the first dungeon room. A trip cord at chest height
carries an ink bladder — auto-detected with Actually Enchanted Glasses.
Disarming it avoids being inked (invisibility lost). Critical path goes
west to Supply Room, then south through Narrow Passageway.

> SOUTH                       [Ink Corridor]
> WEST                        [Supply Room]

Supply Room contains sack of salt, mortar compound, portcullis bar, and
smoke jar (behind Trap 17). With Actually Enchanted Glasses the trap is
detected and disarmed. Take the portcullis bar (needed for Portcullis
Corridor). The salt is only for the idol swap — the idol is a treasure,
not needed for the ring, so leave both. The smoke jar is for Quest 24,
also not needed.

> TAKE PORTCULLIS BAR         [portcullis bar]
> EAST                        [Ink Corridor]
> SOUTH                       [Narrow Passageway]
> SOUTH                       [Idol Room]

Leave the idol on its pedestal — taking it is what springs Trap 33.
Head south to the Combat Room.

SAVE before going into the next room. The fight there can be lost —
death is GAME OVER — and a save lets a player who loses RESTORE and
try again.

> SAVE                        [Saved]
> SOUTH                       [Combat Room]

With Actually Enchanted Glasses the pressure plate at the Combat Room's
threshold is revealed, and we get to choose. The Warden has the
Guardian's Lantern (needed for the Dark Room), and his door to the
Creature Den only opens when the plate fires — so jump on it. The bell
rings, the door opens, and he comes out. One round per KILL; he has 5
hearts and always-max rolls win every round.

> JUMP ON PLATE               [bell]
> KILL WARDEN                 [Warden]
> KILL WARDEN                 [Warden]
> KILL WARDEN                 [Warden]
> KILL WARDEN                 [Warden]
> KILL WARDEN                 [lantern]
> TAKE LANTERN                [Guardian's Lantern]
> SOUTH                       [Prayer Alcove]

Prayer Alcove looks like a dead end until examined. With Actually
Enchanted Glasses it is automatically revealed. Take the crowbar.

> TAKE CROWBAR                [crowbar]

Continue south through Portcullis Corridor. The portcullis carries an
arcane charge — touching without disarming deals lightning damage. With
Actually Enchanted Glasses the charge is spotted and discharged. Lift
the portcullis, then prop it permanently open with the Portcullis Bar
from the Supply Room.
