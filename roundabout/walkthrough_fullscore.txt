ROUNDABOUT: THE GOD-FORSAKEN RING
Full-Score Walkthrough
======================
All active quests, all Trophy Case treasures, all major NPC arcs.
Sections map one-to-one with design requirements; a failing test section
identifies exactly which requirement is broken.

Test runner rules:
  > lines     : feed verbatim to game.do_turn()
  (input: X)  : fed via patched input() during character creation
  [fragment]  : required case-insensitive substring in stdout
  Prose lines : annotation only — ignored by the test runner

This walkthrough uses a Warrior. It gets the Enchanted Glasses in
section B — as early as possible after the opening — before any sailing,
so Kevry can enchant them into Actually Enchanted Glasses before the
rest of the run. Actually Enchanted Glasses pass all perception checks
automatically, removing randomness from the entire playthrough.

Quest coverage:
  Q4  The Whispering Jar
  Q7  The Bone Flute
  Q12 The Locked Music Box (Light spell)
  Q17 The Frozen Watch (Charter for Q27)

  Q27 The Toll Bridge Operator

=============================================================================
A) Opening — White House & Will's Briefing
=============================================================================
Any command except OPEN MAILBOX returns the Zork joke. Opening the
mailbox triggers Will's appearance and character creation.

> OPEN MAILBOX                [mailbox opens]
> warrior                     [You have chosen: Warrior]
> Tester                      [Welcome, Tester]

Will delivers the ring briefing, hands over 10 Zenni, and asks us to
examine the painting to travel to the Tale and Ale.

> EXAMINE PAINTING            [Tale and Ale]

=============================================================================
B) Quest 53 — Will's Glasses (Enchanted Glasses)
=============================================================================
Will's Bedroom is hidden behind a door in the tower Main Room — revealed
by a silent Easy perception check that fires every visit. Return to the
tower via the Tale and Ale mailbox.

> OPEN MAILBOX                [tower]

With always-max rolls the Easy perception check passes immediately.

> NORTH                       [bedroom door]
> ENTER BEDROOM               [nightstand]
> TAKE GLASSES                [wire-rimmed glasses]
> WEAR GLASSES                [glasses]

The glasses are now equipped in the head slot. We will sail to Kevry's
island in section D to have him enchant them. For now, back to the
Tale and Ale to continue setup.

> EXAMINE PAINTING            [Tale and Ale]

C) Quest 51 — The Back Alley Mugger
=============================================================================
The Back Alley is south of The Alley, which is southwest of Town Square.
The mugger fires a perception check on every visit — with Actually
Enchanted Glasses he will be visible on the first visit once glasses are
enchanted, but we have only regular Enchanted Glasses right now and the
always-max mock ensures the check passes.

> SOUTH                       [Town Square]
> SOUTHWEST                   [Alley]
> SOUTH                       [Back Alley]

Perception check passes — mugger visible. Fight to the death.

> KILL MUGGER                 [mugger]

The mugger drops lockpicks. These open the large iron chest in the Mine
Passage (20 Zenni). May's free drink reward is waiting at the Bar.

> TAKE LOCKPICKS              [lockpicks]
> NORTH                       [Alley]
> NORTHEAST                   [Town Square]
> SOUTH                       [Tale and Ale]
> SOUTH                       [Bar]
> TALK TO MAY                 [free drink]
> ORDER DRINK                 [drink]

TT) Ring Binding Ritual — Church of All Altar
=============================================================================
All three ring ritual artifacts: Pale Blade (Brotherhood of the Pale
Blade), Werewolf's Amulet (Veil of the Arcane), Crystal Bowl (Verdant
Circle). Navigate to the Church of All altar.

> EXAMINE PAINTING            [Tale and Ale]
> EAST                        [Main East]
> SOUTH                       [Church Nave]
> EAST                        [Altar]

Place all three artifacts and attune the dial for each. Default
attunement is The Keepers of the Lantern (position 6 in the 7-religion
dial). Brotherhood of the Pale Blade is position 3 — turn left three
times. Veil of the Arcane is position 2 — turn right one time from
position 3. Verdant Circle is position 1 — turn right one time from
position 2.

Place the Pale Blade first:

> PUT PALE BLADE ON ALTAR     [blade placed]
> TURN DIAL LEFT              [dial]
> TURN DIAL LEFT              [dial]
> TURN DIAL LEFT              [white]

The Pale Blade glows white. Place the Werewolf's Amulet:

> PUT AMULET ON ALTAR         [amulet placed]
> TURN DIAL RIGHT             [dial]
> TURN DIAL RIGHT             [red]

The Werewolf's Amulet glows red. Place the Crystal Bowl:

> PUT BOWL ON ALTAR           [bowl placed]
> TURN DIAL RIGHT             [green]

The Crystal Bowl glows green. All three artifacts glowing. Place the
ring:

> WEAR RING                   [ring]
> PUT RING ON ALTAR           [binding]

Binding ceremony fires. All three artifacts consumed. Ring bound.

> TAKE RING                   [bound ring]
> REMOVE RING                 [ring won't go on]
