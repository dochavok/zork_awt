ROUNDABOUT: THE GOD-FORSAKEN RING
Full-Score Walkthrough
======================
This walkthrough plays a Warrior through every active quest, all nine
Trophy Case treasures, and the God-Forsaken Ring, ending at 300 of 300.

Type each line that starts with > at the game prompt. The text in
[brackets] beside a command is part of what the game should say back.
If you see it, you're on track. Lines without a > are notes to help you
along.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A WORD ABOUT LUCK
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike the original Zork, Roundabout has a fair amount of chance in it.
Whether you notice something hidden, win a contest of strength or skill,
or come out of a fight on top can all depend on luck. Your character's
class and level shift the odds as well.

This walkthrough always shows things going your way. If the game's reply
doesn't match the [bracketed] text, luck probably wasn't with you that
time. Nothing is broken. Try again:

  - If something the walkthrough mentions isn't there, leave and come
    back, or wait a moment, and look again.
  - If you lose a contest, try it again. Some opponents want a short
    break before a rematch.
  - Fights can be lost, and death ends the game. SAVE before anything
    dangerous so you can RESTORE and try again.

Don't move on until a step works. Later steps often depend on it.

A tip: the Enchanted Glasses you pick up early on are worth the trouble.
Once they're properly enchanted, very little stays hidden from you.
