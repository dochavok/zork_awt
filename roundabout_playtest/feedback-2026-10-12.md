# Roundabout.exe — Playtest Feedback

## Final summary (end of play)

**How far I got.** I finished the game. As Rowan the Rogue, over about 450 launches, I bound the ring at the Church of All's altar (Veil of the Arcane amulet, Verdant Circle crystal bowl, Brotherhood Pale Blade) and returned it to Will. Final screen: **Score 153 of 300, Level 8, 4 of 9 treasures on display, title "Treasure Hunter."** The four treasures were the Chachapoyan Fertility Idol, the diamond brooch, the Pie Rat Coin and the Forgotten Blade.

What I missed (the last three items were pointed out by the developer after play; see "After play" at the end):
- whatever lies past the skeleton room south of the Antechamber
- the Deep Lock Door
- the Lost Apprentice quest
- **a second island far to the east by ship.** I took "Open ocean in every direction. Nothing else." at face value and turned back.
- **the Flooding Room.** My Rogue auto-disarmed its pressure plate, which leads to a treasure, and the levers gave me nothing to solve them with (one snapped off).
- **the Spirit Room's treasure.** The ring was "too used" by then (see problem 3).

**How much was assisted.** A lot, especially in the second half. The developer hinted these steps, and each is marked ASSISTED in the entries:
- From the earlier sessions:
  - `activate` for the stones, the banner order, and `tip may #`
  - gunpowder for the support, the Assay Room flint, and `up` from Charnel Walk
  - `northeast` to the lighthouse, Will translating scrolls, retrying Lynds, and `north` of the archery range
- From session 4:
  - `jump on plate` (Combat Room) and `use pickaxe on timbers`
  - `clear drain`, the honey heals, and the bridge's hidden weights
  - wearing the heart necklace, and the Town Hall's third floor
  - the Stored Room east of the Mine Passage, and the Pipe Room east of the tavern
  - `press seal` + `dust jar` for the jar, and `mix clay with water` at the fountain
  - `put bowl on pedestal` + `put zenni on pedestal`
  - the Altar room east of the nave, and `put <item> on altar`

Found without help, among others:
- the mirror building and the ghost (ring + Unbind Undead)
- the watch → clerk → charter → Boggart chain
- the Middle Tier Key (via a May tip) and the gravestone
- the aqueduct, the idol swap and the lantern's room
- the Incantation wall
- freezing the pool and thawing the soldier
- the Keeper's office → consecrated stake → werewolf
- the jar's message → fire clay

**Three biggest problems**
1. **Exits and rooms the text never mentions.** This was the single most common reason I got stuck, and it never went away. Never mentioned anywhere:
   - the Tale and Ale's east Pipe Room
   - the Town Hall's third floor
   - the Mine Passage's east Stored Room
   - the Rickety Bridge's north Shrine Room
   - the church nave's east Altar room, which hid the main quest's finale, and which I wrongly reported as an "altar/dial can't be referenced" bug for the whole game
   - the Entrance's south exit in the mirror building

   Most surface rooms don't list exits either. The first half of the dungeon does name its exits, and play there was noticeably smoother. One clause per room ("an archway leads east to the altar") would remove maybe a third of my stuck points.
2. **Single-verb solutions and scenery that isn't an object.** Many key actions accept only one phrasing, and the near-misses get stock replies that suggest the idea is wrong. Examples:
   - `drive stake into werewolf`: attack, stab and use all print "Your blade finds its mark", even with no blade held.
   - `jump on plate` (not step, stand, press or trigger).
   - `place block` (not lift or put).
   - `put zenni on pedestal` (not in bowl, offer or donate).
   - `use pickaxe on timbers`: the crowbar's "You can't get any leverage on that" pushed me away from levering.
   - `clear drain` and `dust jar`.

   Focal scenery named in room text often "can't be seen": the mirrors, pedestal, plinth, the Combat Room door, the rubble, the ceiling, the sluice. `read` often contradicts `examine`, as with the charter's "handwriting" (`read charter` → "nothing written"). Each of these turns a solved puzzle into a guessing game.
3. **Hidden, irreversible state that punishes you later.** Three cases:
   - **The ring's budget is cumulative across wearings, can't be reset, and ends in a *random* game over.** The same `remove ring` from the same save succeeded about 1 time in 3. Each removal says "Whatever it wants, it didn't get it this time", which reads as a clean escape. By the time the Spirit Room needed the ring, wearing it meant game over on the next turn.
   - **The Spirit Room is a trap.** The spirits block the way back as well as forward. With one save slot I'd have been stuck forever; only a manual backup save got me out.
   - **Other hidden rules:** the Rickety Bridge's hidden weights, with the same message however much you drop, turned every trip below the key door into inventory juggling. Several instant deaths come with little or no warning: the bone floor on either exit, the cellar stairs, the skeleton room, Will's glasses.

**Three best moments**
1. **The binding and Will's farewell.** The three lights tracing toward the ring ("The green reaches it first... The white arrives last") and then Will: "I should have told you that at the start. I didn't, because I needed you to go. ... Well done, Rowan." It pays off his cut-off warning from the very first scene.
2. **The Finch family thread and the clerk's watch.** A ghost freed in a hall of mirrors leaves a pocket watch. The clerk recognises his lost uncle's watch and quietly hands over the charter he'd refused me. The charter shifts the Boggart. Hauling Calder Finch's gravestone home earns his grandson's key. The trophy case in the tower bears the explorer's name. It's emotionally grounded, and most of it is solvable.
3. **The rubbing → Archivist → Incantation wall chain**, and the dungeon's best elemental puzzles. The Archivist's "an answer to a question I was never able to find" leads to a wall that "asks you something" and opens "as if it had begun to doubt anyone would ever answer." Close behind: glacier melt freezing the pool and the ivory torch thawing the soldier, the lantern finally finding its hook in the Dark Room, and the Keeper's last note explaining the werewolf.

**Also worth fixing:**
- **Display:** em-dashes print as "�" on Windows, and dropped items sometimes don't appear in room listings (the hand cart, the bowl).
- **Stale text:** the cracked jar after sealing, the gravestone "tip up one edge" after it's upright, the statue, and the forest pedestal after a restore.
- **Names:** the NPC named Rowan shares a common player name, and May's tip read like a bug because of it. "There's no response from the Will Passion" has a stray "the".
- **Ending:** the final banner's "GO BACK TO THE GUILD HALL" refers to something never seen in the game.

## Earlier summary (written after sessions 1–3)

**How far I got.** I played as Rowan, a Rogue, over about 110 launches using SAVE/RESTORE between them. I finished the first errand: I found Pyronicus (by guessing "volcano" from his name), got the ring, and took it back to Will. That opened the main quest: destroy the ring using three of the seven faiths at the Church of All's dial. After that I explored the whole surface map (town, Viking encampment, wasteland, beach and docks, forest, the mine). Along the way I solved Haalvar's riddle, beat the mugger and got lockpicks, rescued the kite and got a rune stone, found the Pie Rat disguise, beat and trained with the knight, and found the ring's invisibility. I died twice (Will's glasses; the bee swarm) and recovered from saves. I stopped when every remaining thread was blocked: the Ritual Circle, the church dial, the statue, the cellar, the church door, the ship, the swarm, and Lynds. Most of them seem to lead to a "lower passages / tunnels under town" area that I never found a way into.

**Three biggest problems**
1. **I couldn't find the way underground, and with it most of the game's progress.** Three NPCs point to the "lower passages" or "tunnels under town", but no entrance is ever named, and every candidate is locked with no hint about how to open it. Unmentioned exits elsewhere (library → Stacks, wasteland → Beach Road) taught me that exits can be hidden, which made the hunt feel like guessing.
2. **The parser keeps refusing the most natural commands.** `x` isn't examine. ASK X ABOUT Y fails for any topic ("ask May about Pyronicus"). `show`, `say`, `answer`, `steal`, `sleep`, `hearts`, and `enter <place>` aren't understood. Focal scenery like the church's dial and altar, the library's card catalogue, and the ritual circle's symbol "can't be seen". Worst cases: the slate literally says "Ask about the stew" and the knight says "Tell him I sent you", and neither can be done.
3. **Key solutions aren't signalled by the scene.** The Ritual Circle gives nothing to work with (silent child, "nothing special" stones). Its only in-world nudge is Ivanaar's one-word "Three", and the element set invites outside knowledge of a cartoon. The knight wants a fight but only invites me to "show" him something. Wearing Will's glasses is an instant, unwarned death. The swarm kills silently.

**Three best moments**
1. **Opening the mailbox and getting Will instead of a leaflet.** "You opened the mailbox... I wasn't entirely certain you would." It's a confident subversion of the familiar opening, followed by Will being cut off mid-warning ("The ring — I should have told you, it—"). Pyronicus's "Will told you what you need to know, I assume" pays that off.
2. **The librarian's stitched-together voice.** She speaks in fragments of a missing researcher's words. It was eerie, and it tied three threads together. The best writing in the game.
3. **Small, warm reactive touches:** the kid flying the rescued kite "badly and happily"; May noticing "the back alley's gone quiet" and buying me a drink; the ring refusing to be set down ("'Bring it to me, or keep it close,' Will said. You keep it close."); the ring growing warm while worn; "Pyronicus built this room first. The volcano came after."

**Session 2 addendum (with developer hints: `activate`, the banner, `tip may #`).** With those hints I finished the Viking Trust Trials. Activating the stones in the banner's order solved the circle; I won the Thornbrew contest and dragged Aylora to Ivanaar, which got me Runed Metal. Pyronicus forged that into the Pale Blade, and Raznak gave me a bow. With the developer's hint about Shamus's stock, I bought gunpowder and wedged it against the mine's split support. I'm stuck again: "A fuse wants a spark" and I've found no spark source. The pickaxe, blade, dagger, and bow all fail to give "leverage" on the statue. A 10-Zenni tip pointed me to the Keeper's locked office in the church, but I have no key. The new lessons: the circle's `touch` reply ("Fiddling... has no effect") actively misled me away from `activate`; the banner clue reads as scenery; and `tip` is undiscoverable from May's "money talks." On whether gunpowder is too obscure: the item isn't, but nothing signals that the support is meant to be destroyed, and the missing spark is the real wall.

**Session 3 addendum (with developer hints: Assay Room south of the Main Shaft; `up` from Charnel Walk).** The flint from the Assay Room set off the mine blast, which turned out to be a diversion that cleared the Pie Rat ship (a great reveal). I took the ship to the island, dug up the treasure (30 Zenni), and got a Pie Rat Coin from the grudging crew. Underground I mapped the Hidden Entrance → Forgotten Shaft → Undercroft → Junction → Bone Passage / Toll Bridge (the Boggart wants 200) → Charnel Walk → Crypt → Mausoleum → Graveyard (behind the church). I died two more times: a flooding door with no warning, and the blast radius, which reaches the Mine Entrance. **New biggest issue:** in the underground, exits are rarely named while the torch timer runs down, so the game punishes the direction-guessing it forces. The Assay Room and "up" to the Crypt were both unfindable for me. I'm stuck again at the Crypt's "far end" passage and the Boggart's toll.

**Also worth fixing quickly:** em-dashes print as "�" on Windows; there's a raw Python traceback if input ends at a prompt; the docks still show the rope after it's taken; my Zenni total jumped unexpectedly once; and the Tip Journal lists the same tip under two quests.
